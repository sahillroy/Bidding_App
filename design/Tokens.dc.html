The house curve. Fast out, settles slowly — reads as weight.
180ms
Colour — borders, text. Fast enough to feel instant.
260ms
Lift and reveal. The card rising, the bid row opening.
420ms
Image scale. Slowest layer — what makes it feel expensive.

The three durations are deliberately different. Move everything on one timing and a hover reads as a switch flipping; stagger them and it reads as an object responding. Under prefers-reduced-motion every duration collapses to 1ms and the transforms are dropped — colour still changes, so the affordance survives.
